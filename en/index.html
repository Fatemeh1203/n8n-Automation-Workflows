A few tips that keep things accurate

Write amounts with dots: 20.000.000. It reads fine without dots too, but dots cut down on mistakes.

Put your own name under "Recorded by." That way the log always shows who made the entry.

Fields marked with a star are required. The form won't submit until they're filled in.

Photograph the receipt or invoice right away. It's much harder to find it later.

Every entry goes to the director first, and lands in the ledger once approved.
